Privacy Policy for Arrowless

Effective Date: September 2026 • Developed by ASN Studios

1. Overview

ASN Studios ("we", "our", or "us") operates Arrowless (Package: com.asnstudios.arrowescape). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you download, install, and play Arrowless.

We are committed to protecting player privacy. Arrowless is designed as an offline-first logic puzzle experience. We do not require you to register, log in, or provide any personal data (such as your real name, email, or phone number) to play.

2. Information We Store Locally

Arrowless stores gameplay progress strictly on your device using local storage (SharedPreferences). This data includes:

  • Progression Data: Highest level reached, level completion status, and stars earned.
  • Cosmetics: Unlocked arrow/board skins and active visual preferences.
  • Settings: Sound effect toggles, haptic feedback toggles, and UI preferences.
This local data never leaves your device and is not synchronized with or uploaded to any external server controlled by ASN Studios. You can erase all local data anytime via the in-game "Wipe Archive" button in Settings.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Arrowless utilizes third-party advertising services provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC) to display advertisements.

Google AdMob may collect and process certain device information in accordance with their privacy practices, which may include:

  • Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID) and device hardware specifications.
  • Network Data: IP address and coarse network location to serve geographically relevant ads.
  • Diagnostics: Performance metrics, ad view interactions, and crash reports.

For more details on how Google processes and safeguards advertising data, please review:

  • Google Privacy & Terms
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

4. Advertising ID & Opt-Out Options

You have control over how your device advertising identifiers are used for personalized ads. You can opt out or reset your identifier anytime directly from your Android device:

  • Open device Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Tap Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.

5. Children's Privacy

Arrowless does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly take appropriate action.

6. Security of Your Data

All communications initiated by integrated third-party SDKs (such as Google AdMob) utilize secure industry-standard HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit.

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
