Privacy Policy

App: Toto Learn: games for Kids (Package: com.asn.totolearn) • Developer: ASN Studios

🛡️

100% Child, Toddler & Family Safe

Toto Learn: games for Kids is designed specifically for children ages 2 to 5. We strictly adhere to COPPA, GDPR-K, and Google Play's Families Policy. We do not collect, track, store, or sell any personal data.

🌟 1. Introduction & Mission

Welcome to Toto Learn: games for Kids (referred to as "Toto Learn", "the App", "we", "us", or "our", Package ID: com.asn.totolearn), created and maintained by ASN Studios.

We believe that digital educational games for toddlers and preschoolers must be wholesome, nurturing, and completely protected from commercial tracking or data harvesting. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to family transparency and details how the App operates in complete privacy.

🚫 2. No Personal Data Collection

We respect the privacy of children and parents. We do NOT collect, store, share, sell, or transmit any personal information whatsoever.

Explicit Guarantee: The App does not prompt for, access, or harvest names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, photos, microphone audio, GPS locations, or device identification numbers (such as Android Advertising ID / AAID).

No user account, profile creation, or social media login is ever required or permitted in Toto Learn.

💾 3. 100% Offline & Local Storage Only

All gameplay data is stored strictly on your local device:

  • Star Balance & Badges: Collected stars and completed levels.
  • Sticker Collection: Unlocked stickers in the digital sticker book.
  • App Settings: Sound effects, background music volume, and motion preferences.

This data is stored purely on the local device using Android's native SharedPreferences storage. This data never leaves your device, is never sent to our servers, and cannot be accessed by third parties. You can clear this data at any time from within the Parent Area or by clearing app data in your Android device settings.

🔕 4. Zero Advertising & Zero Third-Party Trackers

Toto Learn: games for Kids is 100% ad-free:

  • No Banner Ads
  • No Interstitial Popups
  • No Rewarded Video Ads
  • No Behavioral or Analytics SDKs

We do not integrate any advertising networks (such as Google AdMob, Unity Ads, IronSource, etc.) or tracking SDKs (such as Facebook SDK, Adjust, AppsFlyer, etc.). Children will never be interrupted by ads or exposed to marketing content.

🔐 5. Child-Proof Parental Gate

To ensure young children cannot alter app settings or access adult features unintentionally, Toto Learn features a built-in Parental Gate.

Access to volume settings, bedtime screen timers, progress reset, or store management requires solving an interactive randomized arithmetic challenge (e.g., 7 + 6 = ?). This ensures full compliance with the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines and Google Play Families Policy.

📜 6. COPPA, GDPR-K, and Google Play Families Compliance

Toto Learn: games for Kids complies fully with all applicable international child safety and data protection regulations:

  • COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act): We do not collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.
  • GDPR-K (General Data Protection Regulation - Kids): For users in the European Union, no personal data processing occurs.
  • Google Play Families Policy: The App strictly conforms to all Google Play requirements for apps participating in the Designed for Families program.
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) / CalOPPA: No consumer or minor personal information is gathered or sold.

⏰ 7. Bedtime Timer & Digital Wellbeing

Toto Learn includes an optional built-in Bedtime & Screen Time Timer configured by parents within the Parental Gate. This timer runs locally on the device to gently encourage toddlers to wind down and rest when playtime ends. No usage telemetry is transmitted.
