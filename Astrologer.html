04

Local Storage & Technical Security

Astorloger employs the principle of Security by Architecture:

  • App Sandbox Isolation: All profile parameters are stored inside the secure Android application sandbox (SharedPreferences and internal storage), inaccessible to other apps installed on your device.
  • Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3): Any external network requests (such as Google Fonts or asset downloads) are encrypted in transit with strict HTTPS protocol.
  • No Server Database Dependency: Your daily readings and horoscopes do not require storing your birth records in a centralized cloud database.
05

Android Device Permissions

In strict accordance with Google Play's Principle of Least Privilege, Astorloger declares only three standard, non-sensitive Android permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Enables downloading Google Fonts and web rendering components.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Verifies active internet connectivity before attempting asset requests.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Drives tactile haptic feedback when counting beads on the 108 digital Japa Mala.

Zero Sensitive Permissions: We do NOT request Camera, Microphone, Contacts, SMS, Storage, Call Logs, or Precise Location permissions.

User Rights & Self-Service Data Erasure

You maintain unconditional control over your personal data. In accordance with global data protection laws (including Article 17 GDPR Right to Erasure and India DPDP Right to Correction and Erasure):

How to Delete All Data Instantly:

You can immediately wipe all saved records, preferences, birth details, and cached charts by performing either of the following actions:

  1. In-App One-Tap Purge: Open Astorloger → Tap Profile tab → Scroll to "प्रोफ़ाइल रीसेट करें (Reset Journey)" → Confirm. This immediately executes a hard wipe of all local storage keys.
  2. Android System Clear Data: Go to device Settings → Apps → Astorloger → Storage → Clear Data.
  3. Uninstalling the Application: Uninstalling Astorloger completely removes all application sandboxed files from your operating system.
