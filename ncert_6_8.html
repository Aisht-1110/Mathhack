3. Device Permissions & Explanation

To deliver an uninterrupted learning experience, the application requests minimal Android system permissions:

  • INTERNET (`android.permission.INTERNET`): Required solely to stream and download official NCERT chapter PDFs directly from the designated open GitHub repository.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (`android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`): Used to check whether the device has an active Wi-Fi or cellular connection before starting large PDF downloads.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (`android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS`): Used on Android 13+ to send optional daily study practice alerts, quiz challenges, and download completion notifications. Can be toggled on/off in App Settings at any time.
  • VIBRATE (`android.permission.VIBRATE`): Used to deliver gentle native haptic feedback when tapping quiz answers and toggling navigation tabs.

4. Third-Party Services & Content Delivery

The application retrieves publicly licensed textbook materials from our configured GitHub repository (Aisht1110/NCERT). When fetching study files, your device connects directly to GitHub's raw content delivery servers (raw.githubusercontent.com) via standard HTTPS. We do not use any third-party advertising SDKs, marketing trackers, or behavioural analytics frameworks (such as Facebook SDK, AdMob, or Firebase Analytics).

5. Offline Data Storage & Security

All downloaded textbooks and notes are stored within the application's private, sandboxed app directory on your device. No other application on your phone can access your downloaded study materials or reading progress without explicit device root permissions. You can permanently clear all cached materials at any time via the Clean All button in the Downloads screen.

6. Google Play Data Safety Summary

For parents, teachers, and students reviewing our Google Play Store listing, here is our certified Data Safety breakdown:

  • Data Collected: No user data collected.
  • Data Shared: No user data shared with third parties.
  • Data Encrypted in Transit: Yes, all textbook downloads use industry-standard HTTPS TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Data Deletion Request: You can wipe all app data and progress instantly by tapping "Clear Data" in Android App Settings or uninstalling the app.
