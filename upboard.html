Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 11, 2026
Disclaimer: This application ("UP Board Class 9 & 10 Prep") is an independent educational study tool built for students preparing for UPMSP exams. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government entity or state board.

This Privacy Policy describes how UP Board Class 9 & 10 Prep ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Our application is designed to be user-privacy respecting and offline-first:

  • No Account Required: You can use all features of the app without registering, logging in, or providing any personal details (such as name, email address, or phone number).
  • No Personal Data Tracking: We do not ask for or store your personal identification, user profile data, or contact lists.

2. Local Device Storage & Permissions

To provide offline study capabilities, the app requests standard permissions on your Android device:

  • Storage / Media Files: The app downloads NCERT study materials and solutions to your device storage so you can read them offline. These files are stored locally on your phone and can be deleted by you at any time.
  • App Preferences: Your class selection (Class 9 or Class 10), medium choice (Hindi or English), and quiz progress are saved locally on your device via Android shared preferences.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertisements

The app may use third-party services that collect non-personally identifiable information used to identify your device for serving ads and analyzing app performance:

  • Google Play Services: Used to support core Android ecosystem integrations.
  • Google AdMob: We may display advertisements to keep this app free for students. AdMob may use Advertising ID to serve relevant, non-intrusive ads. You can manage or reset your Advertising ID via Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.

For more information on Google's privacy practices, please visit Google Privacy & Terms.

4. Children's Privacy

Our app is intended for secondary school students preparing for Class 9 and Class 10 examinations (typically ages 13 and above). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

5. Security

We value your trust in using our application. Since we do not transmit or store personal data on remote servers, your academic progress and local downloads remain secure on your own physical device.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any updates will be effective immediately upon posting on this page.
