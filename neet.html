Overview & Purpose

This Privacy Policy governs the mobile application (NEET College Predictor / NEET Counselling App) and related web platforms provided by our development team.

Our application assists students in analyzing previous years' NEET UG closing ranks (MCC & State Quotas), comparing medical colleges, and formulating choice-filling preference lists.
