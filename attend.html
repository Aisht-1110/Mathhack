AttendWise Privacy Policy

Your attendance is your business.

AttendWise is built offline-first. Your subjects, attendance and notes stay on your phone. There is no account, no login and no analytics.

Effective 28 Oct 2026 Applies to v1.1.0+ Android
Stored on your device Subjects, records, notes and settings never leave your phone.
No account, no tracking No email, phone number, location or analytics SDK.
You stay in control Export a CSV whenever you want, or erase everything by uninstalling.

1 What AttendWise stores

Everything you create is saved in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Optional profile: a display name and course/semester you may add during setup.
  • Subjects: names, faculty, target percentage.
  • Attendance: dates, status (present, absent, cancelled, holiday), notes and session index.
  • Preferences: theme, reminder schedule, alert settings.
No cloud account We do not run a server that stores your attendance. There is nothing to sign in to, and no one else can read your records.

2 What we do not collect

AttendWise does not collect or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or contacts
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Photos, files or microphone input
  • Usage analytics, crash telemetry or advertising profiles (see section 4 for ads)

Exporting a CSV opens your phone's share sheet. You choose where that file goes; we never receive it.

3 When the app uses the internet

The core app works fully offline. There are only three cases where a network request happens:

  • Update check: up to twice a week (Monday and Thursday) the app downloads a small version file so we can prompt you to install important fixes. This request contains no personal data.
  • Purchases: if you buy AttendWise Pro, the transaction is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see your payment details.
  • Reminders: notifications are scheduled locally on your device; no server is involved.

4 Advertising

Some free versions of AttendWise may display ads served by Google AdMob. When ads are enabled:

  • Google may access a device advertising identifier and approximate technical information (device model, OS version, coarse region) to serve and measure ads.
  • Where required by law (for example in the EEA or UK), you will be shown a consent choice before personalised ads are requested, and you can change it later in the app.
  • Buying AttendWise Pro removes ads.
Current release Ads are not enabled in v1.1.0. This section will apply as soon as an ad-supported update is published.

5 Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. You can remove individual records from the subject calendar, or erase everything by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling AttendWise. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

6 Children's privacy

AttendWise is intended for college and university students and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help you remove it.

7 Security

Your records live in Android's app-private storage, protected by the operating system's sandboxing. We do not transmit them, which removes most interception risks. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so keep your device lock enabled and keep regular CSV exports if the data matters to you.

8 Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change — for example when advertising is introduced — we will update this page and change the effective date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Questions about your data? We usually reply within a few days.
