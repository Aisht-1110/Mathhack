01

Overview

Class 11 & 12 Notes is an independent study app for NCERT and CBSE Class 11 and Class 12 students, published by ASN Studios. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored, and the choices you have.

The short version: the app does not collect personal information, does not require an account, and does not contain ads, analytics or tracking SDKs. Your preferences and downloaded PDFs stay on your device.

This policy applies to the Android and iOS apps and the web preview of Class 11 & 12 Notes (package com.asnstudios.class11_12_notes).
02

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information.

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or date of birth.
  • We do not access your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or personal files.
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not create a user account, profile or advertising identifier.
The only information sent over the internet is the technical request needed to download a PDF or load a typeface. We do not receive or store that information to identify you.
03

Data stored on your device

The app keeps a few things locally so it can remember your choices and work offline. This data stays on your device; we do not have a server copy.

Preferences Local app storage Class, medium, theme and onboarding state
Reading progress Local app storage Bookmarks and the last page you read
Downloads App-private storage Downloaded PDF files and their details
Web preview settings Browser local storage Theme and onboarding preference
04

Internet & third parties

The app uses the internet for:

  • Downloading textbook and note PDFs from files.allncert.in.
  • Opening the content-source link when you choose it in Settings.
  • Loading the Outfit typeface from Google Fonts in some builds.

These services may receive standard technical information such as your IP address and user agent as part of the request. We do not receive or store that information. Their own privacy policies apply.

Third-party content. The PDF files are hosted by a third-party provider. We do not control the host's availability, content or privacy practices. Please review the content host's terms if you visit their website.
05

Downloads & offline files

Downloaded PDFs are saved in the app's private storage. Other apps cannot access that folder.

  • You can delete individual downloads in the Downloads screen.
  • You can clear all offline files from Settings.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its local files and preferences.
06

Ads, analytics & tracking

There are no ads, analytics or tracking in this app.

  • No advertising SDKs or advertising ID.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels or profiling.
  • No third-party marketing or data selling.
07

Permissions

The app requests the Internet permission only.

  • No storage or media permission.
  • No location, camera, microphone or contacts permission.

If you open a PDF in another app, that app may use its own permissions and has its own privacy policy.

08

Children's privacy

Class 11 & 12 Notes is intended for Class 11 and 12 students and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Because the app does not collect personal data or offer accounts, there is no child profile to delete.

09

Retention & deletion

We do not retain personal data on our servers. Local data remains on your device until you delete it:

  • Delete downloads from the Downloads screen.
  • Clear the app's storage in your device settings.
  • Uninstall the app.
  • On the web preview, clear the site data in your browser.
10

Security

Network requests use HTTPS where the host supports it. Downloads and preferences are kept in app-private storage. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but the app is designed to keep your study data on your device.

11

Your choices

You are in control of the app's local data:

  • Change the class, medium and theme at any time.
  • Delete individual PDFs or clear all offline files.
  • Clear app storage or uninstall the app to remove local data.
  • Contact us with any privacy question.
12

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated. If the changes are material, we will make them clear in the app or in the next release notes. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
