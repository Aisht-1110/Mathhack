Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 29, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Math Hacks is designed to operate completely offline. All of your app data—including your game progress, experience points (XP), completed levels, streaks, and unlocked puzzles—is stored locally on your personal device. We do not transmit this data to any external servers.

2. Third-Party Services and Tracking

Because Math Hacks is a premium, ad-free experience, we do not use third-party advertising networks. We also do not use third-party analytics tools that track your behavior across other apps or websites.

Our application is safe for all ages. Because we do not collect any personal data, we do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information (which is technically impossible through the app), please contact us.
