Privacy Policy for Tic Tac Toe

Effective Date: September 2026 • Developed by ASN Studios

1 Overview & Core Commitment

ASN Studios ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how data is handled when you download, install, and play Tic Tac Toe (including Classic 3×3, 4×4, and Infinity modes).

We believe in zero unnecessary data collection. Our game is engineered offline-first. We do not require account registration, passwords, email addresses, phone numbers, or any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) to enjoy the complete game experience.

2 Information We Store Locally on Your Device

All gameplay data and personal settings are saved strictly on your local device (via secure local device storage / SharedPreferences). This data includes:

Match Records

Wins, losses, draws, and win streaks across game modes.

Puzzle Progress

Solved puzzle levels, current difficulty tier, and stars earned.

Custom Themes

Active visual skin selection (Chalkboard, Paper, Dark, etc.).

Audio & Haptics

Sound effect volume, background music toggle, and vibration feedback.

Privacy Assurance: This data never leaves your smartphone or tablet. It is not synchronized with, uploaded to, or transmitted to any remote server owned by ASN Studios. You can wipe this local data anytime by clearing app storage in your device Settings.

3 Third-Party Services & Advertisements

To keep this game free for everyone, we display advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC).

Third-party ad networks may automatically receive technical information when delivering advertisements, which may include:

  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID).
  • Device Diagnostics: Device model, operating system version, and general screen resolution.
  • Coarse Location: Generalized location inferred from IP address (to deliver relevant localized ads).
  • Ad Interaction Metrics: Impressions, clicks, and general ad viewability.

Google’s handling of this data is governed by Google’s own privacy terms:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google Uses Information From Apps That Use Its Services

4 Advertising ID & User Opt-Out Rights

You have complete control over personalized advertising on Android devices. You can reset or disable your advertising identifier at any time:

  • Go to your phone’s Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Tap Reset advertising ID or select Delete advertising ID to opt out of interest-based ads.

5 Children's Online Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Tic Tac Toe is a family-friendly game suitable for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect, request, or store any personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under the digital age of consent in your jurisdiction).

If a parent or guardian discovers that any personal information was unintentionally collected, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly take steps to remove it.

6 Data Security

We prioritize the security of all data. Any network communication initiated by integrated Google SDKs (such as AdMob ad serving) is conducted using modern, industry-standard TLS/HTTPS encryption protocols in transit.

7 Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time (for instance, to reflect regulatory updates or new app features). Any revisions will be published here with an updated effective date.
