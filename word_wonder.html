1

Our Commitment to Children's Privacy

At ASN Studios ("we", "our", or "us"), protecting the privacy of young learners and families is our top priority. Word Wonder is an educational application created for children of all ages, including early learners under the age of 13.

We strictly follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation for Kids (GDPR-K), and the Google Play Families Policy. We do not require children to share any personal information to use any feature of this app.

🌟 The ASN Studios Kid-Safe Promise

Word Wonder never asks for names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, GPS locations, audio recordings, or payment card details from your child.

2

Information We Do NOT Collect

To maintain an uncompromising standard of privacy, Word Wonder collects zero personal data:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): No real names, addresses, birthdays, or emails.
  • No Precise or Approximate Location: We never request GPS or network location.
  • No Camera or Microphone Access: Tracing is done with fingers; speech is generated locally using on-device Text-to-Speech (TTS).
  • No Device Identifiers or Fingerprinting: No Advertising IDs (AAID), IMEI, MAC address, or serial numbers are accessed or stored.
  • No Third-Party Analytics: No third-party behavioral trackers (such as Google Analytics for Firebase, Facebook SDK, or Adjust) are present in the app.
  • No External Advertisements: There are no commercial advertisements, sponsored content, or in-game tracking.
3

Local Device Storage Only

To save game state between play sessions, Word Wonder stores minimal gameplay settings locally on the player's physical device using standard Android local storage (SharedPreferences):

  • Player Nickname: An optional nickname or avatar chosen inside the app (e.g., "Superstar") to personalize greetings.
  • Educational Progress: Stars earned, completed crossword words, mastered alphabet letters, and daily streak counts.
  • Sticker Collection: Digital stickers unlocked and placed on the in-app sticker album canvas.
  • Parental Preferences: Sound toggle, daily reminder alert time, screen time limiter duration, and Ollie voice accent preference (US, UK, Australia, India).
🔒 Device-Bound Storage

All game data remains exclusively inside the app's private sandbox on your phone or tablet. It is never uploaded to external servers, cloud databases, or shared with third parties. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app deletes all local data immediately.

4

Permissions Requested

Word Wonder requires zero dangerous Android permissions.

  • No Internet Permission: The app operates 100% offline.
  • No Storage Permission: The app does not read your photos, documents, or external files.
  • No Contact or Phone Permissions: The app has zero access to address books or phone states.
5

Parent Zone & Parental Controls

We empower parents and educators with full control over the learning experience:

  • Parental Gate: Settings, certificates, and screen limits are protected behind an adult multiplication gate to prevent unintended child access.
  • Screen Time Limiter: Parents can set a play timer (e.g., 15, 30, or 45 minutes) that gently reminds the child to take a healthy rest break.
  • Voice Accent Selector: Parents can configure Ollie the Owl’s speech synthesis to their preferred English dialect (US, UK, Australia, India).
  • Reset & Delete: Parents can reset all game progress or delete local settings at any time directly through the Parent Zone or through Android system settings.
6

Compliance with Global Privacy Standards

Word Wonder complies with all applicable privacy regulations worldwide:

  • Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA): We do not collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / GDPR-K): Because we process no personal data, no data subject profiling or cross-border data transfer occurs.
  • Google Play Families Policy: Our app meets all criteria under the Google Play Designed for Families and Teacher Approved programs.
7

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage parents to review this page periodically. Continued use of the application indicates acceptance of any updated policy terms.
