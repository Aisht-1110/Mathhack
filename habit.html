1 Information We Collect

We only collect data that directly serves your experience. Nothing extra, nothing hidden.

💡 Local-first architecture. Most of your data stays on your device. We only sync what you explicitly choose to back up. You can use Vitals entirely offline.

Data You Provide

  • Account info — Email address and display name (if you create an account; local-only mode requires none)
  • Health entries — Habit logs, check-in responses (energy, pain, spoons), mood notes, trigger tags, and partial/skip reasons you enter
  • Condition preferences — Selected condition pack(s) (e.g., ADHD, Chronic Pain), routine templates, and reminder schedules
  • Optional profile — Time zone, typical wake/sleep times (used to schedule reminders), care-team sharing preferences

Data Collected Automatically

  • Usage analytics — Screen visits, feature interaction frequencies, crash reports (anonymized; see Section 4)
  • Device info — OS version, app version, device model (for compatibility and bug fixing)
  • Notifications — Delivery receipts and open timestamps (solely to improve reminder reliability)

What We Never Collect

🚫 Hard boundaries — no exceptions

We never collect precise geolocation, contacts, photos, browser history, financial information, or biometric data. We never build advertising profiles. We never sell personal data under any circumstances.

2 How We Use Your Information

Every data point we handle maps to a specific, beneficial purpose. We don't collect first and find uses later.

🎯 Adaptive routine engine

Your check-in data (energy, symptoms, spoons) powers the adaptive engine that adjusts your daily habit list — scaling down on flare days, reordering by energy level, and protecting your streak when you log a valid skip reason. This processing happens on-device whenever possible.

📊 Insights & consistency scoring

We calculate your consistency score, streaks, and trend patterns locally. These help you see patterns over time — like which triggers precede flares or which habits correlate with better days. No data leaves your device for this.

☁️ Cloud backup & sync (optional)

If you create an account, your data syncs via end-to-end encrypted channels so you can restore it on a new device. We cannot read your health data in transit or at rest. The server only sees encrypted blobs.

🔔 Reminders & notifications

Reminder schedules you set are processed locally. If you enable cross-device sync, push notification tokens are stored server-side solely to deliver your reminders. We track delivery/open rates only to fix reliability issues.

🛠️ App stability & improvement

Anonymized crash reports and aggregate feature-usage counts help us fix bugs and decide where to invest development effort. Usage data is stripped of all personal identifiers before it reaches our analytics provider.

🧠 ADHD-friendly by design. We don't use dark patterns, infinite scroll metrics, or engagement-maximizing hooks. Our analytics measure whether features help — not how long we can keep you in the app.

3 Storage & Security

On-Device Storage

All health data you enter is stored in your device's secure enclave / encrypted local storage. This means:

  • Data is encrypted at rest on your device
  • Other apps cannot access it
  • It remains available offline
  • You can delete it at any time by clearing app data

Cloud Storage (Account Holders Only)

If you opt into cloud backup:

  • Encryption — AES-256 at rest; TLS 1.3 in transit. We use end-to-end encryption so even our infrastructure team cannot read your data
  • Hosting — Data is stored on SOC 2 Type II certified infrastructure within the EU and US
  • Access controls — No employee has routine access to user data. Break-glass procedures require VP-level approval and are audit-logged
🔐 Zero-knowledge health data

Your health entries are encrypted with a key derived from your password. We don't hold the decryption key. Even if our servers were compromised, your health data would remain unreadable ciphertext.

5 Your Rights & Choices

You always have the final say over your information.

📤 Export all your data

Navigate to Settings → Your Data → Export. You'll receive a ZIP file containing all your health entries, check-ins, and account data in open JSON format. No lock-in, no delays — export completes within seconds.

🗑️ Delete your data

Local data: Clear app data or uninstall the app — it's gone immediately.
Cloud data: Settings → Your Data → Delete Account. This permanently erases all synced data from our servers within 30 days (typically within 48 hours). Deletion is irreversible.

✏️ Correct or update information

You can edit any health entry, check-in, or habit at any time within the app. Account details (email, name) can be updated under Settings → Account. There is no limit on edits.

🔒 Restrict processing

You can disable the adaptive engine (Settings → Adaptive Engine → Off), which stops all automated adjustments to your routine. Your data will still be stored but not processed for personalization. You can re-enable it at any time.

🚫 Opt out of analytics

Settings → Privacy → Analytics. Toggle off to stop sending anonymized usage data. This does not affect core functionality. Crash reports can also be disabled separately.

👤 Use Vitals without an account

Vitals works fully in local-only mode. No email, no cloud, no account needed. You'll miss cross-device sync and backup, but all core features — habits, check-ins, adaptive engine, insights — work entirely on-device. You can upgrade to a cloud account later without losing data.

7 Children's Privacy

Vitals is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that a child under 13 has created an account, we will delete it and all associated data promptly.

For users aged 13–17, we recommend parental guidance. Condition management can involve sensitive health decisions, and we encourage families to discuss routine tracking together.

8 California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know — Request a complete list of data categories we've collected, the business purpose, and any third parties we've shared it with (past 12 months)
  • Delete — Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of sale — We do not sell personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of. We include this right for completeness
  • Non-discrimination — Exercising any privacy right will not result in different treatment or degraded service
✅ No sale, no sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising. In CCPA terms, we are a "no sale" business. The "Do Not Sell" link is not needed because there is nothing to opt out of.

To exercise any CCPA right, email privacy@vitals.app or use the in-app Settings → Privacy → Data Request. We respond within 45 days (30 for verified requests).

9 GDPR for EU/EEA Users

If you are in the European Union or European Economic Area, you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation:

  • Right of access — Obtain confirmation and a copy of your personal data
  • Right to rectification — Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erasure — Request deletion ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restriction — Limit how we process your data
  • Right to data portability — Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to object — Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to withdraw consent — Revoke any consent you previously gave, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing
